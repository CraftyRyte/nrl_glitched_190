The robot

Pixel

A 2.5 kg differential-drive robot that began as the NRL kit. Everything it can do beyond manual driving, we added.

ComponentsSpecificationWhat we addedPrototypesDriver aids

Annotated view

What you are looking at

  1. A fixed-plane arm that sweeps a 90° envelope, from stowed to scoring height. The driver sets its angle with the right stick.

  2. Toggles between two commanded positions, open and clamped. Its profile is cut around the 95 mm CORE ring.

  3. Two digital servos, a Robokits RKI-1248 (20 kg·cm) and a Robotics Dynamix RDS-3225, driven directly by the Hub.

  4. An ESP32-S3 controller. It runs our C++ firmware and the link to the driver station.

  5. Four Johnson geared motors, 12 V and 300 RPM, two per side, turn 10 cm higher-grip wheels on set-screw hubs.

  6. Bolted 3D-printed clamps pass the drive torque into the base plate, so a motor cannot twist in its mount under load.

  7. 3 mm laser-cut acrylic plates on aluminium standoffs keep the frame light and the centre of mass low.

On top of the kit

What we added

The kit arrived as a drivable chassis with a servo arm and gripper, under manual control only.

  • DrivetrainA Grade A drivetrain: higher-grip tyres now, with quadrature encoders planned for P3.
  • Line sensingAn 8-channel infrared array with closed-loop line following.
  • TurningInertial measurement for turns, with a named fault stop.
  • Driver aidsPrecision and turbo modes, scripted turns and an emergency stop.
  • FirmwareOur own C++ on the HEXA Command Hub.

Driver aids and repair

Built to be driven and fixed

  • Two speedsPrecision mode for scoring approaches, turbo for crossing the open field.
  • Scripted turns90° and 180° turns are closed-loop routines on the face buttons, so accuracy does not depend on driver feel.
  • Emergency stopOn a shoulder button, reachable without a thumb leaving either stick.
  • Quick repairSet-screw hubs and bolted clamps allow a wheel or motor swap in roughly 20 minutes, with hand tools and a soldering iron.
  • Before each matchThe infrared array is recalibrated on the venue mat to allow for its surface.
