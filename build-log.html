Build log

What broke, and what we did

Eight build sessions, from 17 October to 11 November 2026. Each entry is one chain: goal, problem, options, fix, result.

The portfolio records the order of the work, not the date of each fix, so entries run in build order.

17 Oct 2026First build session

P1

Manual kit

Kit chassis with arm and gripper. Tank drive and servo arm by controller. No line sensor.

Decision

300 RPM or 600 RPM

Goal
Push and carry reliably at 2.5 kg, under the 8 kg limit.
Options
300 RPM, at about 300 N-cm stall torque in seller listings, or 600 RPM at about 122 N-cm.
Decision
Kept 300 RPM. At 2.5 kg, traction limits pushing, not torque. Fitted new wheels for grip and quality.
Result
About 1.57 m/s at full power, calculated from 300 RPM and 10 cm wheels.
Fixed

Motors twisting in their mounts

Problem
Motors can twist under load.
Fix
Designed bolted 3D-printed clamps. The wheels sit on set-screw hubs.
Result
The motors stay fixed under load.
Partly working

The arm: reach against stability

Goal
Lift the 95 mm CORE ring and release it on a pole or in the Drop Zone.
Problem
Reach and stability pull against each other. A longer arm scores higher but shifts the centre of mass.
Options
A 180° rotating arm was rejected on stability and driver-workload grounds.
Fix
A fixed-plane arm sweeping a 90° envelope, with a two-position gripper profiled to the ring.
Result
Driver-controlled pick-up works. Automated pick-and-drop is planned, not yet tested.
Fixed

The robot drove backwards

Problem
Drive direction was reversed.
Fix
Inverted the left-side sign in firmware.

P2

Line following

P1 plus the 8-channel infrared array and the inertial unit. PID line following with a junction stop.

Fixed

A typo stopped the build

Goal
Follow the line with PID on the 8-sensor array and stop at the first junction.
Problem
The build stopped on a typo: TankDrve instead of TankDrive.
Fix
Corrected it and rebuilt.
Fixed

The Hub could not see the line sensor

Problem
The Hub did not detect the line sensor. The array was not enumerating.
Fix
Traced it to Hub firmware and reflashed the HEXA Command Hub.
Result
Stops at the right junction in 3 to 4 of 6 to 7 runs.
Measured

New tape, new calibration

Goal
Stay centred on the line and know our real speed.
Problem
On new tape the robot stayed centred only after recalibrating.
Decision
Recalibrate at every venue, because new tape needed it.
Fix
Re-ran LINE TEST and wrote a Speed Test OpMode for timed 3 s and 5 s runs.
Result
1.88 m in 3 s and 3.11 m in 5 s, about 0.61 m/s.
Partly working

The inertial unit read 1.6× too far

Goal
Drive known distances for the pick-and-drop route.
Problem
The inertial unit read about 1.6× too far.
Options
The inertial unit, tape-measured distance, or encoders.
Fix
Tape-measured distances for now. The inertial unit is used only for turns, with a gyro guard: about 60° for a 90° turn and about 150° for a U-turn.
Result
Distances come from tape. The full-route test is still to do.
11 Nov 2026Eighth build session. Everything below is planned.

P3

Encoders

P2 plus ME-28 encoders and full mission code. Planned: not yet fitted or tested.

Planned, not tested

Distance from the wheels

Goal
Measure distance from the wheels.
Problem
The kit motors have no encoder, and rule R5.3 forbids modifying motors.
Plan
Asked the organisers, then ordered motors and ME-28 encoders. They plug into the motor driver board, clear of the multiplexer pins.
Result
Not yet fitted or tested.
Planned, not tested

Closed-loop odometry

Goal
Drive to a commanded distance from wheel counts, with gyroscopically guarded turns.
Options
Keep taped references as the fallback, or move entirely to encoder counts.
Plan
Calibrate counts per metre on a measured run, then replace the taped distances step by step.
Result
Nothing yet. Taped references stay in the program until the encoder route is proven.
Planned, not tested

Battery and the full route

Goal
Confirm that line speed holds across the battery's state of charge, then prove the whole route.
Problem
All timings so far come from a freshly charged pack, so late-match behaviour is unknown.
Options
Log pack voltage against measured speed, or cap the commanded speed with margin.
Plan
Repeat the timed 3 s and 5 s runs at full, mid and low charge, then run each mission step 3 to 5 times.
Result
Nothing yet. Planned timings stay below 0.61 m/s until this is measured.
Open action

Over-current protection

Problem
No inline fuse is fitted. The main switch is the only isolation point.
Plan
Add over-current protection.
