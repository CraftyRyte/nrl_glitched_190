The game plan

We play an offensive game and rarely defend, because contact inside the opponent's perimeter (G4.8) and de-scoring (G4.5) both carry penalties.

The field

Where the points are

The Hyperdrive field. Source: NRL game manual, fig. 3.0.

  1. 3 points for each CORE, four to a pole. Our scoring priority.

  2. 1 point for each CORE. Held as the fallback when a pole is full or out of reach.

  3. Final 30 seconds: Balance for 15 points, or Dock for 8.

  4. Where the Human Player feeds CORE into play.

Scoring priority is CORE on High Poles, with the Drop Zone held as a fallback. Star Poles score 5 for each CORE and an Apex Pole scores 9.

Plan by phase

Three phases

  1. Phase 01

    Autonomous

    Leave the box, follow the line, pick and drop CORE. The first junction is proven. The remainder is in test.

  2. Phase 02

    Driver control

    Cycle CORE to High Poles, and use the Cargo Bay to unlock Starburst early. It takes 10 CORE; Payload cuts that to 4.

  3. Phase 03

    Endgame

    Final 30 seconds: Balance on the Space Station for 15, or Dock for 8. An Apex Pole is 9 each.

Who does what

  • DriverOnly drives and scores.
  • Human PlayerFeeds the Source Zone and presses Starburst.
  • Drive StrategistCalls time at 1:00 and 2:00.

If the match changes

  • Pole full or out of reachPut CORE in the Drop Zone for 1 point, as a backup.
  • Opponent in our wayDrive around. Never make contact inside their perimeter (G4.8).
  • Under 30 s leftGo to the Space Station: Balance for 15 if Pixel can, otherwise Dock for 8.
  • Fault in autonomousThe robot stops and shows the fault name.

Tuning order

  1. 1 DriveStraight-line trim, left and right sides matched.
  2. 2 Control gainsPID steering, one variable at a time.
  3. 3 JunctionsDetection threshold and debounce.
  4. 4 TurnsGyroscopic guard angles for quarter turns and reversals.

How we measure a run

  • Each step is repeated 3 to 5 times. A step passes only when it repeats.
  • Telemetry is logged from the driver station: fault state, junction count, line error.
  • Open action: repeat the speed test across battery states of charge.

If something goes wrong

  • Any latched fault, the emergency stop or a lost link cuts drive power.
  • A carry flag enforces single-CORE transport.
